## Safety behavior

The renderer is owner-scoped and opt-in-only.

The renderer refuses disabled saved searches.

The renderer does not deliver email.

The renderer does not mutate:

- `last_notification_checked_at`
- `last_notification_sent_at`

The renderer is not imported by the saved-search scheduler command in this checkpoint.
