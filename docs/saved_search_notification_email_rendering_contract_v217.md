## What v217 defines

v217 defines the required contract for a future saved-search notification email renderer.

A future renderer must prepare:

- Recipient email.
- Recipient username.
- Saved search id.
- Saved search name.
- Saved search querystring.
- Match count.
- Subject prefix.
- Plain text body.
- HTML body, if templates are introduced later.
- Saved-search management link.
- Clear unsubscribe or notification-management guidance.

## Contract requirements

The future renderer must be owner-scoped and opt-in-only.

It must not render or send for saved searches where email notifications are disabled.

It must use the existing notification fields:

- `email_notifications_enabled`
- `last_notification_checked_at`
- `last_notification_sent_at`

It must not update `last_notification_sent_at` until a later explicit sending checkpoint exists.

It must not send email from the renderer contract checkpoint.
