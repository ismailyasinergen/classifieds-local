When to suspend seller

Use temporary seller suspension only for serious or repeated risk.

  • Repeated scam or fraud reports
  • Asked buyer to pay outside the platform
  • Harassment or abusive behavior
  • Impersonation or fake identity
  • Suspicious business/verification identity
  • Multiple misleading or suspicious listings

Action guide

  • Dismiss: weak, false, duplicate, or revenge report.
  • Mark reviewed: valid but no direct punishment needed.
  • Warn seller: first/low-risk violation.
  • Remove verification: identity or trust issue.
  • Archive listings: seller has multiple risky listings.
  • Block messaging: harassment or off-platform pressure.
