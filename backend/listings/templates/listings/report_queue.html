When to temporarily suspend listing

Use this when buyers may be harmed while admin investigates.

  • Scam or fraud signs
  • Illegal/restricted/prohibited item
  • Counterfeit item
  • Strong misleading title, price, or photos
  • Multiple reports on the same listing
  • Contact/payment instructions that bypass platform safety

Action guide

  • Dismiss: weak, false, duplicate, or revenge report.
  • Mark reviewed: report is useful but listing stays active.
  • Suspend listing: hide temporarily while investigating.
  • Archive listing: confirmed bad listing or should be removed.
